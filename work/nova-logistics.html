Nova Logistics

PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Challenge

Nova Logistics managed thousands of global shipping nodes using disjointed regional databases and manual routing. Unpredictable events like weather or port delays cascaded through their network, causing massive systemic inefficiencies and inflated operational costs.

System Strategy

We developed a centralized, AI-driven routing engine that processes live telemetry and satellite data. By containerizing regional infrastructure into a global Kubernetes cluster, the system dynamically reroutes shipments at the edge before delays occur, visualizing the entire operation through a high-performance interactive dashboard.
