PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Challenge

Quantum Finance operated on legacy risk-modeling systems that could not process the volume of live market data fast enough. Their analysts were forced to make decisions on data that was already 15 to 30 minutes stale, resulting in missed alpha and elevated exposure.

System Strategy

We replaced their entire batch-processing architecture with a real-time event streaming pipeline. By normalizing fragmented data inputs at the edge and passing them through a custom TensorFlow inference layer, we delivered sub-second predictive insights directly to a new, high-performance analyst dashboard.
